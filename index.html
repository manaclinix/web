Who we are

Connecting Africa to world-class clinical research.

ManaClinix brings together patients, sponsors, CROs, healthcare systems and regulators through trusted partnerships, regional expertise and technology to make clinical research across Africa more ethical, inclusive and effective.

Our mission

We connect people, expertise and technology to make clinical research across Africa more ethical, inclusive and effective.

We bring together the people and capabilities needed to make high-quality clinical research more accessible across Africa, while putting patients at the centre of the research experience.

Our vision

To transform Africa into a globally recognised hub for patient-centred, world-class clinical research.

A connected research ecosystem where patients, partners, infrastructure and technology work together to strengthen clinical research across the continent.

What we do

Building a connected future for clinical research.

ManaClinix connects patients, healthcare systems, industry partners and regional expertise to expand access to medical innovation, strengthen clinical research and build sustainable healthcare innovation across Africa.

We build trusted patient networks and connected health data to help patients access relevant research opportunities and innovative medicines, while enabling faster, more representative participant recruitment.

We connect healthcare innovators with healthcare systems, regulators and trusted local partners, providing regional insight and coordination to support responsible market entry and access across African healthcare markets.

Through regional expertise and strategic advisory support, we work with healthcare systems and partners to strengthen research infrastructure, regulatory frameworks and local capabilities that enable sustainable clinical research and healthcare innovation.

We develop patient-centred technology that improves access, communication and participation while connecting patients, healthcare providers and research partners to support more efficient clinical trial delivery.

For Patients

Clinical trials should feel transparent, not confusing.

Patients are at the centre of ManaClinix. We aim to make clinical research easier to understand and access, with clear information, informed choice and respect for every participant throughout the research journey.

Why consider participating in clinical research?

Explore Research Opportunities Learn about clinical research opportunities that may be relevant to you and understand what participation would involve before deciding.
Contribute to Better Evidence Participation can help research better reflect diverse populations and generate evidence that is more relevant to patients across Africa.
Contribute to Medical Progress By taking part in research, participants can contribute to knowledge that may inform the development of future medicines, treatments and healthcare.
Make an Informed Choice Receive clear information about the study, including what participation involves, so you can decide freely whether taking part is right for you.

What Patients Can Expect

ManaClinix is designed around clarity, dignity and trust. We aim to help patients understand research opportunities and support informed, voluntary participation while keeping patient welfare at the centre of the experience.

  • ✓ Clear and understandable study information
  • ✓ Support in navigating the research journey
  • ✓ Informed and voluntary decision-making
  • ✓ Respect for participant rights, dignity and privacy

Participation in a clinical trial does not guarantee access to a particular study, treatment or medical benefit. Eligibility depends on the requirements of each study, and investigational treatments may not be effective and may involve risks. Study-specific information should always be reviewed with the relevant research and healthcare team before deciding whether to participate.
