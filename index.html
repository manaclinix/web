Who we are

Connecting Africa to world-class clinical research.

ManaClinix brings together patients, sponsors, CROs, healthcare systems and regulators through trusted partnerships, regional expertise and technology to make clinical research across Africa more ethical, inclusive and effective.

Our mission

We connect people, expertise and technology to make clinical research across Africa more ethical, inclusive and effective.

We bring together the people and capabilities needed to make high-quality clinical research more accessible across Africa, while putting patients at the centre of the research experience.

Our vision

To transform Africa into a globally recognised hub for patient-centred, world-class clinical research.

A connected research ecosystem where patients, partners, infrastructure and technology work together to strengthen clinical research across the continent.

What we do

Building a connected future for clinical research.

ManaClinix brings together patients, partners, expertise and technology to make clinical research across Africa more accessible, efficient and patient-centred.

We build and connect a patient database of potential participants to make access to relevant clinical research opportunities easier and help accelerate trial delivery.

We connect sponsors, CROs, healthcare systems and local partners across the trial journey, supporting coordinated delivery from feasibility and recruitment through to execution.

Through consultancy and regional expertise, we help strengthen the infrastructure, regulatory frameworks and capabilities needed to support world-class clinical research across Africa.

We develop technology that makes clinical research more accessible, connected and patient-centred while helping sponsors, CROs and research teams facilitate trial delivery.

For Sponsors

A faster, more reliable path to African trial execution.

Sponsors need speed, quality, ethical confidence and local insight. ManaClinix helps create a more efficient path from feasibility to recruitment and execution.

Why conduct trials in Africa

Diverse Populations Broaden representation in clinical research and strengthen external validity.
Patient Access Accelerate patient recruitment to include heterogeneous patient populations.
First-Mover Benefit Secure an early leadership position as Africa's clinical research market expands.
Health Equity Expand opportunities for African patients to participate in innovative research.

Sponsor Value

We reduce complexity while preserving quality, helping sponsors access trusted local coordination, ethically sound recruitment pathways and a clearer view of African trial feasibility.

  • ✓ Faster feasibility and site activation
  • ✓ Trusted local regulatory insight
  • ✓ Higher-quality patient recruitment
  • ✓ One partner across the trial journey

For CROs

Local coordination and operational support across African sites.

CROs benefit from regional expertise, stakeholder alignment and a network that helps make trial delivery more efficient and more locally grounded.

Why register with ManaClinix

Pan-African Reach Connect with opportunities across African markets through a coordinated regional network.
Local Partnerships Access site intelligence and trusted relationships within local healthcare ecosystems.
Operational Support Strengthen site coordination, patient pathways and trial execution.
Technology-Enabled Collaboration Support structured communication and coordination across sites, partners and trial teams.

CRO Value

ManaClinix helps CRO teams work more effectively across diverse African markets through regional expertise, local coordination and a dependable collaboration model.

  • ✓ Operational support across African sites
  • ✓ Local partnerships and site intelligence
  • ✓ Streamlined communication and coordination
  • ✓ Technology-enabled trial execution

For Patients

Respectful participation. Clear information. Better access.

Patients are at the centre of our model. We aim to make clinical trials understandable, ethical and supportive so participants feel informed and respected.

Why participate in clinical trials

Access Research Opportunities Learn about opportunities to participate in carefully run clinical research.
Contribute to Better Evidence Help clinical research better represent diverse patient populations and healthcare needs.
Support Medical Progress Participation can contribute to research that advances understanding of medicines and treatments.
Informed Participation Receive clear study information so you can make an informed and voluntary decision.

Patient Value

ManaClinix supports a participant experience grounded in clarity, dignity and trust, with patient welfare and informed participation at the centre of clinical research.

  • ✓ Clear, respectful study information
  • ✓ Support through the recruitment journey
  • ✓ Access to carefully run research opportunities
  • ✓ Protection, consent and dignity first
