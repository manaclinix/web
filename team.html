Founders

Gift is a DPhil candidate in Oncology at the University of Oxford, where his research sits at the interface of tumour mechanics, intracellular signalling, and and their potential relevance to cancer detection and treatment. He holds an MSc in Applied Cancer Science from Oxford, and his background spans applied biosciences, biotechnology, cancer biology, computational analysis, and translational research. A Rhodes Scholar and Presidential Award recipient, he is also co-founder of a biotechnology initiative in Southern Africa and an active contributor to science communication, entrepreneurship, and scholar-led programming. His work is driven by a commitment to building rigorous, ethical, and locally relevant health innovations that connect scientific discovery with real-world patient and community needs.

Luisa is pursuing a PhD in Immunology at the University of Oxford on the Rhodes Scholarship. Her academic education encompasses medical research, global governance, and communication studies (BSc Molecular Medicine, BA Rhetoric and Latin, MSc Global Governance and Diplomacy). Alongside her studies, she is the founding President of the Oxford Society for Science Communication and Engagement. Her particular interest lies in patient-centred clinical research, with a focus on making the patient experience central to how research is designed, communicated and delivered.
